## **"Flow" - The Anticipatory Decision Engine**

**TL;DR:** A decision-making app that uses AI to present choices BEFORE you ask, lets you swipe on micro-factors (not spreadsheets), and externalizes your internal voices (Disciplined Me vs Lazy Me) to make decisions feel like a 5-second game instead of exhausting work.

**Why it's different:** Anticipatory (comes to you), Factor-based (not option-based), Voice-driven (emotional not logical), Offline-first (sub-100ms), Psychology-backed (loss aversion + micro-dopamine).

**The vision:** "Lunch ready? Ramen or Pizza?" appears on your lock screen at 11:30 AM. You swipe through factor cards. Done in 10 seconds. The app becomes your micro-coach.

---

### **Core Philosophy: Built for Myself First, Then Others**
This app solves MY decision fatigue problem. If it works for me daily, it will work for others.

### **The Core Problem: Why Every Decision App Fails**

**High Friction = Instant Abandonment**

❌ **1. Too Much Overhead**
- Long forms, too many steps, forced evaluations of 10 factors
- When you're mentally tired, a questionnaire is the LAST thing you want

❌ **2. They Feel Like Work**
- Decision apps are mini-spreadsheets disguised as helpers
- You don't want extra work—you want relief

❌ **3. No Emotional Reward**
- Humans act emotionally, not logically
- "How productive do you feel?" → boring
- "Hell yes, choose this!" → dopamine hit

❌ **4. No Hook to Return**
- Require consistency but give rewards too late
- Aren't fun, people quit early
- The loop isn't satisfying

❌ **5. They Don't Adapt**
- Treat you the same on good days, tired days, anxious days
- One-size-fits-all approaches don't work

---

### **The Solution: Anticipatory UX + Zero-Input Decisions**

**The Revolutionary Shift:**
Instead of "What do you want?" → The app presents "Ramen or Pizza?" before you even ask.

**Core Value Proposition:**
An anticipatory decision engine that learns your patterns, presents binary choices at the perfect moment, and makes deciding feel like a satisfying micro-game instead of exhausting work.

---

### **What Makes "Flow" Fundamentally Different**

**1. ANTICIPATORY (Not Reactive)**
- The app comes to YOU with options already prepared
- Uses context (time, location, history) to predict what you need to decide
- Example: 11:30 AM → "Lunch today: Ramen or Pizza?" (not "What do you want for lunch?")

**2. ZERO-INPUT REQUIRED**
- No typing, no forms, no thinking
- Just swipe left/right on pre-generated cards
- Sub-5-second interactions

**3. CONTEXT-AWARE**
- Knows if you're tired, overwhelmed, or energized
- Adapts recommendations based on your state
- Learns your patterns: when you're weak, when you're disciplined, when you need a push

**4. CONVERSATIONAL (Not Clinical)**
- Feels like a friend texting you advice
- "nah man don't buy that" vs "Based on analysis..."
- Playful → serious personality range

**5. MULTIPLE "VOICES" SYSTEM** 🔥
- Add your own internal personas:
  - "Disciplined Me" (strict, goal-focused)
  - "Lazy Me" (wants comfort, avoids effort)
  - "Future Me" (long-term thinking)
  - "Chaotic Me" (YOLO energy)
- Each voice gives different advice on the SAME decision
- YOU swipe through their perspectives

**6. OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE**
- Decision feed syncs in background
- When you open app, cards are already there
- No loading spinners (87% of abandonments happen during 2-sec delays)
- Sub-100ms interaction times

**7. POSITIVE REINFORCEMENT ONLY**
- No guilt, no shame
- Every decision gets micro-dopamine hits (confetti, haptics, fun messages)
- Loss aversion through "Decision Betting" (bet points on streaks)

**Step 1: Anticipatory Trigger**

- Open app → cards already loaded (offline-first)

- Card 2: "Pizza"

  - Swipe right = yes, left = no
- Card 3 (optional): "Factors to consider"
  - "You've had pizza 3x this week" → swipe left
  - "You said you wanted to save money" → swipe left
  - "It's raining and ramen is perfect" → swipe right

**Step 4: Instant Result + Action**
- "RAMEN WINS! 🎉"
- Confetti animation, haptic feedback
- "Open delivery app" button → deep link to order

**Total Time: Under 10 seconds**

### **Core Features (MVP)**

**1. ANTICIPATORY DECISION FEED** (The Game-Changer)
- AI generates decisions based on:
  - Time of day ("11:30 AM = lunch time")
  - Location ("Near your gym = workout today?")
  - Calendar ("Meeting in 30 mins = coffee or nap?")
  - Weather ("Raining = stay in or brave it?")
  - Patterns ("Friday night = go out or Netflix?")
- Push notifications with binary choices
- Deep links from notifications to instant decision cards

**2. MULTIPLE VOICES SYSTEM** 🔥
- Create 3-5 internal personas
- Each voice "swipes" on decision factors
- Examples:
  - **Disciplined Me:** "You've wasted 2 hours today already" → No Netflix
  - **Lazy Me:** "You worked hard, you deserve rest" → Yes Netflix
  - **Future Me:** "You'll regret this tomorrow" → No Netflix
  - **Chaotic Me:** "YOLO, live a little!" → Yes Netflix
- Final decision aggregates all voices
- Conversational tone: "bro you NEED a break" not "Analysis suggests rest"

**3. FACTOR-BASED SWIPING** (Not Option-Based)
- Instead of swiping on "Option A vs Option B"
- You swipe on MICRO-FACTORS:
  - "You haven't treated yourself in 10 days" → Swipe right (yes, this matters)
  - "Your budget is tight this week" → Swipe left (no, ignore this)
  - "Most people regret this purchase" → Swipe right (yes, consider this)
- App aggregates your swipes to make final decision
- Feels like a game, not an evaluation

**4. DEFAULT TIMER (Loss Aversion Hack)**
- Every decision card has visible countdown (e.g., 2 minutes)
- If you don't swipe, app auto-chooses the "Default" option
- Default is usually the healthier/productive choice
- You swipe to AVOID being forced into default
- Overcomes decision paralysis through urgency

**5. DECISION BETTING (Gamification)**
- Earn points for each decision made
- Bet points on streaks: "I bet 50 points I'll clear my deck by 10 AM"
- If you fail, you LOSE points (and avatar loses gear/status)
- Loss aversion >> rewards for behavior change
- Leaderboard with friends (optional)

**6. SOLO + COLLABORATIVE MODES**
- **Solo:** Just you and your voices
- **Decision Buddy:** Invite partner/friend to add their voice to your decision
- **Group Mode:** Multiple people swipe, democracy or veto power
- Real-time sync of swipes and results

**7. ULTRA-FAST INPUT (When You Add Manual Decisions)**
- Voice: "Should I buy this €50 jacket?"
- Photo: Screenshot product, app extracts details
- Link: Paste URL, app pulls product/restaurant info
- Template: "Weekend plans", "Big purchase", "Relationship choice"

### **Platform Strategy: Mobile-First (React Native)**

**Decision: Native Mobile App (iOS + Android) - No PWA**

**Rationale:**
1. **Swipe Gestures Are Mobile-Native**
   - Core UX (Tinder-like swiping) feels natural on mobile, awkward on desktop
   - Touch gestures, haptic feedback, smooth animations are what make this engaging
   - Desktop users don't naturally "swipe" - breaks the core experience

2. **Target Market Lives on Mobile**
   - Gen Z/TikTok generation primarily uses phones for apps
   - Mobile-first apps feel more legitimate and "real" to young users
   - App Store presence = discoverability & credibility

3. **PWA Limitations**
   - Limited haptic feedback support
   - Worse animation performance (critical for smooth swipes)
   - No App Store/Play Store presence (major discovery channel)
   - Janky push notifications on iOS
   - Can't monetize via in-app purchases (huge limitation)

4. **Expo Makes Cross-Platform Easy**
   - Build once, deploy to iOS + Android
   - No need for separate web version to reach users initially
   - Web version can come later if needed

**Launch Strategy:**
- TestFlight (iOS beta) + Google Play Beta for initial users
- App Store launch for growth
- Optional web dashboard later for desktop viewing/management

  - **Weekend:** Go out, Netflix, Gym, Read, Side project, See friends
  - **Work:** Focus work, Take break, Check emails, Meeting prep

**Phase B: Making a Decision (Tournament Style)**
1. Select decision type: "What should I eat?"
2. App shows your 7 food options
3. **Tournament Bracket:**
   - Round 1: Pizza vs Ramen → you swipe through factors → Ramen wins
   - Round 2: Ramen vs Salad → you swipe through factors → Ramen wins
   - Round 3: Ramen vs Burger → Burger wins
   - Continue until 1 winner emerges

4. **Factor Swiping (for each battle):**
   - Card 1: "Does this align with 'Save money'?"
     - Pizza: €15 vs Ramen: €12 → swipe right for Ramen
   - Card 2: "Does this align with 'Eat healthier'?"
     - Pizza: swipe left, Ramen: swipe left (neither great)
   - Card 3: "Already ate junk food today?" (from your routine)
     - If yes → healthy option gets boost
   - Winner advances to next round

5. **Final Result:**
   - "BURGER WINS! 🎉"
   - Save to history with timestamp
   - Update preference profile (Burger +1 point)

**Phase C: Preference Learning (Organic)**
- Each time an option wins, it gets +1 point
- Next time you choose "Food", options sorted by preference score
- Your favorites bubble to the top naturally
- After 10 decisions, you see patterns: "You choose Ramen 60% of the time"

**User Stories (You = User):**
- As me, I can configure my goals and routine ONCE (takes 5 mins)

**Simple Database Schema (WatermelonDB):**

```
Table: goals
- id
- name (e.g., "Save money")
- priority (1-5)
- created_at

Table: routine_checks
- id
- question (e.g., "Already ate junk today?")
- type (boolean, number, text)
- value (current value, resets daily/weekly)

Table: option_pools
- id
- category (Food, Weekend, Work)
- options (JSON array: ["Pizza", "Ramen", "Salad"...])

Table: voices
- id
- name (e.g., "Disciplined Me")
- priority_goals (JSON: goal IDs this voice cares about)
- weight (1.0 = normal, 2.0 = double weight)
- phrases (JSON: ["Stop wasting time", "You know better"])

Table: decisions
- id
- category (Food, Weekend, Work)
- winner (e.g., "Ramen")
- runner_up (e.g., "Pizza")
- timestamp
- voice_breakdown (JSON: which voice won most battles)

Table: preferences
- id
- category
- option_name (e.g., "Ramen")
- score (wins count)
- last_chosen
```

### **Sprint 1-2 (Weeks 2-3): "Voices" System (Manual, No AI Yet)**

**Goals:**
- Add the killer differentiator: Multiple Voices
- Make it FUN and ADDICTIVE
- Still no AI - you configure voice personalities manually

**How Voices Work (Simplified):**

**Setup (One-Time):**
- Create 3-5 voices (pre-configured templates available)
- **Disciplined Me:**
  - Priority: Long-term goals, discipline, productivity
  - Tone: Strict (manually write a few example phrases: "Stop wasting time", "You know better")
- **Lazy Me:**
  - Priority: Comfort, rest, ease
  - Tone: Chill (example phrases: "Bro you deserve a break", "Chill out")
- **Future Me:**
  - Priority: Long-term consequences
  - Tone: Wise (example phrases: "You'll thank yourself later", "Think ahead")
- **Budget Me:** (optional)
  - Priority: Saving money
  - Tone: Practical (example phrases: "That's too expensive", "Save for later")

**During Tournament Battles:**
- Each voice gets a "vote" on factor cards
- Example battle: Pizza vs Ramen
  - Factor: "Does this align with 'Save money'?"
  - **Disciplined Me:** Ramen (cheaper, responsible)
  - **Lazy Me:** Pizza (more satisfying, less effort)
  - **Future Me:** Ramen (building good habits)
  - **Tally:** Ramen gets 2 votes, Pizza gets 1 vote → Ramen wins this factor

**Voice Weight System:**
- You can set voice priority (e.g., Disciplined Me = 2x weight, Lazy Me = 1x)
- Context-based auto-weighting:
  - Morning (6 AM - 12 PM): Disciplined Me gets 2x weight
  - Night (10 PM - 2 AM): Lazy Me gets 0.5x weight (prevent bad late-night decisions)
  - Weekend: Lazy Me gets 1.5x weight (it's okay to relax)

**User Stories:**
- As a user, I can create 3-5 voices with names and priorities
- As a user, I can use pre-configured voice templates (Disciplined, Lazy, Future, Budget, Chaotic)
- As a user, during each tournament battle, I see each voice's "opinion"
- As a user, I can manually override the voices if I want
- As a user, voices help me externalize my internal debate
- As a user, I see which voice "won" most often in my decision history

**Deliverables:**
- Voice creation/management screen
- Pre-configured voice templates (5 templates)
- Voice voting system (each voice weighs in on factors)
- Voice weight configuration (priority settings)
- Time-based auto-weighting (morning vs night)
- Results screen shows: "Disciplined Me won 3/5 battles"
- Decision history with voice breakdowns
- NO AI yet (just weighted voting based on factor alignment)

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Creating voices takes <2 minutes (using templates)
- Voice voting feels like you're debating with yourself
- Time-based weighting actually changes outcomes (test it!)
- You use the app daily and DON'T get bored
- Friends ask "what's that app?" when they see it

**Why No AI Yet:**
- ✅ You can build this in 1-2 weeks
- ✅ Voting logic is simple (factor alignment + weights)
- ✅ Still creates the "multiple voices" magic
- ✅ AI can come later to generate voice phrases/advice (Sprint 7-8)
